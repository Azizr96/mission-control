Mission Briefing

Here's how a focus session works:

  1. Add your missions to the list, and mark one as current.
  2. Press Start to begin a 25-minute Pomodoro focus session.
  3. When it ends, take a 5-minute Short Break.
  4. After 4 Pomodoros, you'll get a longer 15-minute Long Break.
  5. Check missions off as you complete them — your daily stats update automatically.

Durations are adjustable any time under "Mission durations," and everything is saved automatically so you won't lose your progress on refresh.
